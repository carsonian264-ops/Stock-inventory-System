⚠️ Couldn't Confirm Past Tables
We couldn't read your previously archived daily tables, so closing the day was paused rather than risk overwriting them. This usually means one of two things:

• This is genuinely your first time closing a day — nothing's archived yet, so continuing is safe.
• You've closed days before and this is a temporary connection issue — if so, continuing would erase those earlier archives. If you're not sure which applies, choose Retry.
